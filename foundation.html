THE FOUNDATION

Every universe begins with an idea.

ODAVEN began with a simple belief held by a visionary — the belief that imagination could become something greater than the imagination itself.

He got a vision, and he decided to build something extraordinary; something he could look back on years and be grateful that he had the courage to dream.

He began building a place where creativity could breathe, where stories could unfold, where opportunities could be discovered, and where odavens could become part of something they could help shape.

What began as a single thought became a vision.

That vision became a purpose.

And that purpose became ODAVEN.

From a single thought comes a world.

That world is becoming a universe.

This is where ODAVEN began.
